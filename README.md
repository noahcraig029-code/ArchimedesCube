## The Archimedes Cube 

Hey guys! This is the Archimedes Cube, a giant scaled-up Rubik’s Cube that I CADed on Onshape to have marble maze tiles on the faces! This lets you generate millions of different marble maze combinations that you can play through so if you decide to build it, you’ll never get bored. I spent a lot of time designing and manufacturing this, and it was a way bigger construction project then I expected!

You should have:

* 1 Core 
* 6 Swivels 
* 8 Corners 
* 12 Sides 
* Maze Panels 
* Edge/Corner Framing Pieces

I recommend sanding down wherever the pieces are going to touch to avoid too much tension. If your cube is struggling to turn, you should try turning the corner pieces opposite each other so that you get a cleaner rotation. make sure nothing gets in between the pieces and gunks up the inside, otherwise things could get really messy.
