## Archimedes Cube — Stardance
Welcome to the repository for the Archimedes Cube. This project is a customizable, 3D-printable puzzle cube featuring modular maze faces. Once fully assembled, a marble can travel across the entire outer shell from panel to panel.
------------------------------
## Component Checklist
Ensure you have all the necessary components printed before beginning assembly:

* 1 Core — The central structural foundation.
* 6 Swivels — The center mechanism pieces.
* 8 Corners — Outer corner puzzle pieces.
* 12 Sides — Outer edge puzzle pieces.
* Maze Panels — External tracks for the marble.
* Edge/Corner Framing Pieces — Outer tunnel walls for marble transit.
* Optional Magnets — For internal alignment and marble storage.

------------------------------
## Assembly Instructions
Follow these steps carefully to ensure smooth rotation and clear paths for the marble.
## 1. Pre-Processing and Sanding
Sand down each individual piece before starting the assembly. Removing printing artifacts reduces friction and ensures the cube turns smoothly.
## 2. Core and Base Layer Assembly
Insert the 6 swivels into the central core. Slide the side pieces into the grooves created by the swivel bases. If you are using magnets, double-check that their poles are correctly oriented to attract the magnet pieces before securing them.
Insert the corner pieces in between the side pieces. Assemble the cube layer by layer, starting with the bottom and moving to the middle.
## 3. Top Layer Alignment
Build the final layer up, following the structure of a traditional Rubik's cube. Do not put a corner or side piece in last, as forcing them into place can damage the internal structure. Always insert a center swivel piece last to ensure a tight, efficient turn and clasp.
Before gluing anything further, turn the un-mazed cube around for a bit to break in the plastic and verify that the mechanism rotates properly.
------------------------------
## Attaching the Mazes
Once the base cube turns well, you can mount the external maze tracks.

   1. Secure the Panels: Use liquid superglue to fasten the maze faces onto the center swivels. Take care not to accidentally glue the moving layers of the cube together.
   2. Test the Tracks: Place a marble on the tracks to ensure it travels from panel to panel without getting stuck or slipping out.
   3. Install Tunnels: Attach the edge pieces with the curved tunnel facing each respective panel tunnel. Once three edge pieces meet on a corner, you can place a nub into the gap on the corner of the three edges.
   4. Customization: Print the design tiles to place them into the green, blue, orange, or red middles, or create your own custom variations.
   5. Storage Plug: Add magnets to the yellow middle section and print the custom maze plug. This provides a secure storage slot for the marble while you solve the mechanical puzzle.
